5m — The surface interval

A small dive centre with an outsized reef on its doorstep.

The Andaman Islands sit where the Bay of Bengal meets the open Indian Ocean — a scatter of granite peaks and fringing reefs far enough from the mainland to stay clear, warm, and wild. We run small groups, dive the sites at their best hour, and treat every descent like the privilege it is.

18m — What lives down here

The reef is crowded, in the best way.

Andaman reefs are among the least-dived in Asia, and it shows. Schools thicken, big animals cruise close, and the coral still builds. On a good day you'll lose count of the species before you reach the safety stop.

Green & hawksbill turtles Manta rays Reef sharks Napoleon wrasse Barracuda schools Clown & anemonefish Ghost pipefish Nudibranchs Bumphead parrotfish
