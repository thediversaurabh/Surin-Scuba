Dixon's Pinnacle

A submerged seamount rising from the deep in a series of tiered rocks — the Andamans' headline dive, and its best chance at the big stuff.

LevelAdvanced

Johnny's Gorge

A sandy channel between coral heads that acts like a magnet. Sit still on the sand and let the parade — rays, sharks, giant groupers — come to you.

Depth20–30m
LevelAdvanced
CurrentModerate
StingraysGroupersSweetlips

The Wall · Neil Island

A reef edge off Neil that slopes then falls into blue, draped in soft coral and gorgonians. Turtles rest on the ledges; eagle rays pass in the current.

Depth12–28m
LevelIntermediate
CurrentMild–mod
Green turtlesEagle raysSoft coral

Nemo Reef

A gentle, shallow coral garden alive with anemones and their clownfish. Calm, bright, and forgiving — our favourite first ocean dive and a macro paradise.

LevelAll levels
CurrentGentle

The Aquarium

Exactly what it sounds like — a shallow bommie field so thick with snappers and fusiliers you'll swim through curtains of fish. Endless for photography.

LevelAll levels
CurrentGentle

Jackson's Bar

A long rocky ridge swept by nutrient-rich current, drawing dense schools and, in season, the occasional manta banking through the blue above.

Depth18–30m
LevelIntermediate
CurrentModerate

Minerva's Delight

A cluster of coral bommies rising from sand — a treasure hunt of the small and strange. Ghost pipefish, frogfish, and nudibranchs reward a slow diver.

Depth14–24m
LevelIntermediate
CurrentMild
FrogfishGhost pipefishShrimp

Lighthouse

A shallow house reef that transforms after dark. Torch-lit corals, hunting lionfish, sleeping parrotfish, and if you're lucky, a bioluminescent finale.

Depth6–18m
LevelAll levels
CurrentGentle
LionfishCrabsBioluminescence
