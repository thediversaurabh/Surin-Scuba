A small centre that takes the reef personally.

Surin Scuba was born from a simple frustration: the Andamans hold world-class diving, and too few people get to see it done properly. So we built the dive centre we'd want to dive with.

2009 — Where it started

Fifteen years of the same short boat ride, and it still thrills us.

Founder-instructor Rohan Menon came to Havelock for a season and never left. What began as a single instructor and a borrowed compressor is now a tight team of PADI professionals who dive these reefs year-round — and still argue about which site is best.

We stayed small on purpose. Small groups mean we can read the water, change plans for a passing manta, and actually know your name by the second dive. It's the difference between being processed and being guided.

Safety — the quiet foundation

The best dives are the ones where nothing goes wrong.

Safety isn't a poster on our wall — it's the routine that lets you relax and look up at the fish.

  • Serviced, checked equipment

    Every regulator, BCD, and cylinder is logged, serviced on schedule, and checked before it touches the water.

  • Emergency-ready

    Oxygen and first-aid on every boat, EFR-trained guides, and a documented evacuation plan for the islands.

  • Conservative profiles

    We dive within limits, plan generous safety stops, and never let a schedule push a dive that shouldn't happen.

Reef-first

The reef doesn't need us. We need the reef.

A share of every booking funds local reef monitoring, and we teach every diver the habits that keep coral alive: perfect buoyancy before depth, hands to yourself, reef-safe sunscreen only, and nothing taken but photographs.
